# Concerns

Topics people who don't code often forget. Ask in plain words, one at a time, only the ones that apply.

For a personal app (one user, one device), most of these don't apply. "When things go wrong" always does.

- **People and roles**: who else uses it? Can some people do more than others (owner, staff, guest)?
- **Accounts and login**: do people need to sign in? What if they forget their password?
- **What's stored, and privacy**: what personal information does it keep? Who can see it? Health, money or children's data can bring legal rules: check them with a subagent.
- **Money**: does it take payments? What will it cost to run each month (hosting, services)? Is there a budget?
- **Devices**: phone, computer, or both? Must it work without internet?
- **Languages**: one language or several?
- **Accessibility**: can people with poor sight, or who only use a keyboard, use it?
- **When things go wrong**: what if data is lost, someone makes a mistake (undo? backups?), or someone misuses it?
- **How many people**: roughly how many users now, and in a year?
